# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

> A comprehensive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built in **Power BI** to analyze workforce trends, employee attrition, demographics, departmental performance, salary distribution, and employee satisfaction. This dashboard helps HR professionals and business leaders make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention and workforce planning.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution designed to monitor key Human Resource metrics. It provides a complete overview of the organization's workforce by tracking employee count, attrition rate, age distribution, salary slabs, job roles, department-wise performance, gender diversity, experience trends, and job satisfaction.

The dashboard enables HR managers to identify the primary causes of employee attrition, understand workforce demographics, monitor employee engagement, and optimize HR strategies.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall workforce statistics.
- Monitor employee attrition across departments.
- Measure employee retention performance.
- Identify high attrition job roles.
- Analyze employee demographics.
- Compare salary slabs with attrition.
- Track employee experience trends.
- Measure job satisfaction across different job roles.
- Support strategic HR decision-making.

# 📈 Dashboard Features

## 1️⃣ Employee Summary

Provides a quick overview of:

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Employee Age

---

## 2️⃣ Department Filter

Interactive department slicer allowing users to analyze data by:

- Administration
- Finance
- Human Resources
- IT
- Marketing
- Operations
- Sales

---

## 3️⃣ Age Group Filter

Analyze employee information across different age categories:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

---

## 4️⃣ Attrition by Department

Visualizes employee attrition across various departments.

Helps identify:

- Highest employee turnover
- Low retention departments
- HR improvement opportunities

---

## 5️⃣ Attrition by Salary Slab

Analyzes attrition based on salary ranges.

Useful for identifying:

- Salary-related resignations
- Compensation improvement areas
- Employee retention strategies

---

## 6️⃣ Job Role & Job Satisfaction Matrix

Displays employee attrition based on:

- Job Role
- Job Satisfaction Level
- Total Attrition

Helps understand which job roles experience the highest dissatisfaction and employee turnover.

---

## 7️⃣ Age Group Distribution

Shows employee count across different age groups.

Useful for:

- Workforce planning
- Talent acquisition
- Succession planning

---

## 8️⃣ Attrition by Gender

Compares employee attrition between:

- Male Employees
- Female Employees

Supports diversity and inclusion analysis.

---

## 9️⃣ Attrition Trend by Experience

Tracks employee attrition based on years of experience.

Helps identify:

- Early resignations
- Experienced employee turnover
- Retention trends

## 🔟 Department-wise Employee Count

Displays the total number of employees working in each department.

Useful for:

- Workforce allocation
